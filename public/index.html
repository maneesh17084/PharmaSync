Features

Everything a medical distributor runs on

From the goods-received note to the tax invoice and the payment that settles it, every step updates stock and leaves an audit trail.

Batch-wise stock & FEFO

Every batch keeps its own mfg/expiry date, cost and MRP. Orders draw from the batch that expires first and spill over to the next one.

Expiry & low-stock alerts

Near-expiry and expired batches are flagged and blocked from sale. Low stock comes with a suggested order and a one-click purchase order.

GST tax invoices

PDF invoices with place of supply and the right split: CGST + SGST (or UTGST) within the state, IGST across states. GSTINs are validated.

Customer payments

Record full, partial or later payments with mode and cheque/UTR reference. Balances, receivables and payment history stay exact.

Purchases & suppliers

Purchase orders, goods received with batch details, direct shipments and supplier payments, all posted to stock automatically.

Roles & audit trail

Admin and staff roles enforced in the business layer, BCrypt passwords, and a log of every login, change and stock movement.

Get started

Up and running in two minutes

No database server, no setup wizard. PharmaSync creates its own data file on first start.

Install Java 21

Any JDK or JRE 21, for example Eclipse Temurin.

Download

Get pharmasync.jar from the latest release.

Run it

Double-click the jar, or run java -jar pharmasync.jar. Choose demo data to explore.

Sign in

Use admin / admin123, then pick a new password and enter your company details.
